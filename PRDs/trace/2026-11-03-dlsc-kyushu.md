## 2. Contradictions

1. **Past submissions as input.** C2: "Past graded submissions are not an input." DLSC past-assignment mode uploads the teacher's past grade and comment and rates the AI against them (S31 F1, Target workflow); `T-Import` already maps "Past grade / Past comment"; Kyushu's 26 Oct run generated the rubric from "the lecture text **and a teacher's corrected examples**" and judged the tone by them. Either C2's exclusion is narrowed to "not an anchor for generation" or the import fields and Kyushu's practice are out of spec.
2. **Pass/Resubmit proposal and weights vs no-score rule.** C3 feedback shape: "No score, grade or level unless a scoring product asks for one"; C2: rubric weights removed, settled. S31 F2/F3 show an AI **Pass/Resubmit** proposal per report with the teacher's choice recorded, F6 gives each rule a weight "counts toward Resubmit", F8 measures pass/resubmit agreement. C11.16 never lists this as a K-row. A per-report verdict is a two-level grade; PRD must either admit it for the teacher-only mode or tell DLSC it is out.
3. **"Returned elsewhere" status.** C2 says the teacher-only queue has it; C3's status checklist (Not Reviewed · In Review · Returned · Sent Back · Auto-returned · Resubmitted · Failed "eighth") omits it; C6 has no AC; no board shows it. S31's four-step flow (To review → Reviewed → Comment copied → Done in KULeD) also needs "Comment copied" as an event (D17).
4. **Which PRD owns the Correspondence track.** A2 (3 Nov) assigns teacher-only import to AI Feedback and says "E1's partner list gains the Kindai Correspondence track"; but C2 Out of scope still sends "rule-checked grading assistance at Correspondence Division scale" to the AI Grading PRD, C7 still says "KULeD's API ingest and write-back requirement belongs with the Correspondence track, in the AI Grading PRD", C11.16's opening keeps the pre-ruling text ("A scope ruling is needed first … nothing from them is written into C2 or C3"), and C2 says the mode's screens "are drawn after the Okayama bid and the DLSC contract are decided" while C11.17 N11/N12 record them as drawn. Stale text in four places.
5. **Coverage rule vs unjudgeable criteria.** C3: "Every criterion gets exactly one item" with verdict flag/ok. Kyushu's items 3 (7) 清書 and 3 (6) 原稿用紙の使い方 may not be judgeable from a photo (26 Oct Q1 — the 清書 card appeared every time with unrelated content). C3's OCR row only lets a **figure** be "reported as unread". A third verdict value (cannot judge / unread) or a per-item "AI does not judge" flag is needed, otherwise the coverage rule manufactures false verdicts.
6. **Read-check assumes a student.** C3 OCR row: "the student can correct them before asking for feedback". In the teacher-only mode there is no student; handwritten or scanned PDFs from KULeD would arrive with nobody to confirm spans. Rule needs a teacher-side variant (unconfirmed spans shown on `T-Review`, excluded from findings).
7. **External ID semantics.** `T-Import` maps "External ID (KULeD ID)" to `student_id`; S31 keys everything by a per-**report** ID (R-24117) and asks which ID may be shown; C2 says "keyed by an external ID" without saying report or student. Re-upload-updates (D6) and resubmission linking depend on which it is.
8. **Export names.** C7: "names optional (DLSC drops them)"; `T-DashAI` export shapes all carry "Student Name" with no switch.
9. **DLSC tone "do not hand over the answer"** is answer level 1 by C3, but C3 also says Kindai's free-text questions get level 1 "only when the student asks" (sociology). For DLSC there is no student to ask; the level applies to the draft. Not a conflict, but C3's answer-level row should say the level governs the teacher draft in teacher-only mode.
